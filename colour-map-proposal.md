# Colour map: a PROPOSAL, not a binding

Proposed by BOSS 2026-09-02. **Not frozen.** channel-token.md makes CONTROL-SOFTWARE
the definitional end of the colour axis and says the eight are bound once, in one
table, when the carrier set is known. **This is a proposal for that binding, and the
carrier availability answer is still owed by the owner through DOSING.**

## The finding that shapes the whole proposal

The owner's constraint was: no colour already meaning something else in that enclosure.
Applied honestly, **that constraint eats most of the palette.**

In a panel, several colours are already spoken for by function on the conductors
themselves: protective earth, neutral, line, and DC polarity. **BOSS states no standard
from memory and asserts no reservation as fact** - which colours are reserved, and by
what document, is a lookup. **But the direction is not in doubt: a channel colour
competing with a function colour on the same conductor is a defect either way.**

**So the resolution is to move the axis rather than to shrink it.**

> **THE CHANNEL COLOUR IS CARRIED ON THE MARKER, NOT ON THE CONDUCTOR INSULATION.**
>
> The insulation carries FUNCTION, which is what an electrician reads. The marker
> carries CHANNEL, which is what an operator reads. **They are two different questions
> asked of the same wire by two different people, and giving each its own carrier is
> what lets both be answered.**

Three things fall out of that and all three are wanted:

- **T-011 stops competing with the colour axis.** T-011 says two conductors in one
  jacket cannot share a colour. Under G-29 a signal is paired with its own return, so
  the two conductors of one channel's pair are in one jacket and DO share a channel.
  **On the insulation that is a collision. On the marker it is correct**, because the
  pair is one channel and the marker says so.
- **The owner's constraint is satisfied exactly rather than approximately:** no marker
  colour can mean something else in the enclosure, because markers do not carry
  function anywhere in this build.
- It matches what the declaration already asks for: **colour-through or otherwise
  non-printed carriers**, which is a marker technology, not an insulation one.

## The proposed eight

Constraints applied, in order of how hard they bind:

1. **CH5 and CH6 maximally separated.** They are the pH pair and they are adjacent
   tokens, F-063. The declaration requires non-neighbouring colours.
2. **Distinguishable to a colour-blind operator.** Separation on the blue-yellow axis
   and on lightness survives every common colour-vision deficiency; separation on the
   red-green axis does not.
3. **No two adjacent tokens both neutral**, so a dirty or dim marker cannot slide from
   one to its neighbour by lightness alone.
4. Green excluded entirely, on the reservation above.

| Token | Colour | Why it sits there |
|---|---|---|
| CH1 | white | |
| CH2 | brown | breaks the neutral run |
| CH3 | grey | |
| CH4 | red | breaks the neutral run |
| **CH5** | **BLUE** | **pH. Maximally separated from CH6 on the blue-yellow axis AND on lightness, which is the pair of axes that survives every common colour-vision deficiency. This is the strongest separation available in any eight-colour set** |
| **CH6** | **YELLOW** | **pH. As CH5** |
| CH7 | black | |
| CH8 | violet | |

**Green is not used.** Red and green never appear as a pair, which is the classic
collapse.

**Both segments of one channel share that channel's colour**, per the owner's
constraint: the suction and delivery runs, both ends of each, the head, the station and
the jug body all carry the same marker colour. **The colour says WHICH CHANNEL. It
never says which end, which segment or which direction** - those are read from the
token and from position.

## What is honest about this proposal and what is not

**Honest:** the CH5-versus-CH6 binding is the strongest that exists, and it is the one
that matters, because it is the pair F-063 is about and the pair whose confusion is
invisible to everything except the direction-aware check.

**Weaker than it looks:** CH1, CH3 and CH7 are white, grey and black, which are three
points on one axis. **They are not adjacent tokens, which was the constraint, but under
poor light and nutrient residue they are the three most likely to be confused with each
other.** The declaration already says colour is never the sole differentiator anywhere,
and the number and position axes carry the identity alongside it. **That is the
mitigation and it is stated rather than assumed.**

**Not established:** whether eight colours exist in a colour-through, non-printed
carrier that survives acid, base and generic nutrient-concentrate duty, D-084. **If the
available set is smaller than eight, this table is rebuilt around what exists and the
CH5 and CH6 binding is protected first.** That is the owner's lookup, through DOSING.

**Not decided by BOSS:** the binding itself. This is a proposal to CONTROL-SOFTWARE,
which owns the axis, and to the owner, who owns the carrier.
