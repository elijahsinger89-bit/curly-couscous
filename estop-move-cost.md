# THE COST OF MOVING THE FIVE 22 mm DEVICES FROM THE TOP FACE TO THE DOOR

**Requested by the owner 2026-09-23 against F-122. NOTHING HAS BEEN STARTED. This is
the cost, for the ruling.**

**The owner's reasoning, recorded because it is the argument and not the ruling: an
E-stop that requires an overhead reach is not an E-stop; and the top face is the
upward-facing penetration that sets the assembly's rating regardless of what is
gasketed on it. Five gasketed devices on a horizontal face is a rating bought and then
spent on the one surface that collects standing water. D-047's reasoning was about
sealing, the consequence was operability, and the consequence was never weighed
against it.**

## 3. WHAT IT COSTS THAT THE TOP FACE DID NOT

**ONE NEW COLLISION, AND IT IS THE EXACT COLLISION SHEET 3.6.3 SAID DID NOT HAVE TO
HAPPEN.**

**On the top face the device bodies hang DOWN from the roof into the door-side volume,
which is empty.** 3.6.3 bought that clearance by choosing where along the depth axis to
drill: **"the clearance is bought by where the hole goes, not by moving a rail."**

**On the door the bodies hang BACKWARD, toward the plate.** The door-side volume stops
being empty because the devices are now in it, and what they point at is B1 to B4 -
the supply, the contactors, the relays and the terminals.

| | |
|---|---|
| Body depth behind the face | **43 mm** at one row of blocks, **55 mm** at two, **68 mm** at three. RESET needs at least two NO blocks and a third if it proves the lamps |
| Box depth | **201 mm** overall |
| **What is NOT on file** | **The clear depth from the closed door's inner face to the mounting plate.** Not in parts.md, not in D3 |

**SO THE MOVE NEEDS ONE LOOKUP BEFORE IT CAN BE DRAWN**, and it is the same kind of
figure as the mounting-hole patterns: **measurable off the box in hand, faster than a
listing.**

| Wanted | How |
|---|---|
| **Clear depth, closed door's inner face to the mounting plate's front surface** | **Measure it.** Close the door on a soft stop and measure, or measure the flange depth and subtract the plate standoff |
| Search term, if not measured | `Qilipsu 508 x 419 x 201 enclosure door to backplate depth` |

**And one thing that is NOT a new cost: the door opens, so the bodies swing with it.**
Nothing behind them has to be reachable past them. **A device on the door is easier to
service than one hanging from a roof, not harder.**

## 4. WHAT IT COSTS THAT IS NOT DRAWING

| | |
|---|---|
| **Door-mounted devices' conductors flex every time the door opens** | The five devices' wiring becomes a **flexing loop**, which a roof-mounted device's never was. It wants a service loop and a strain path, and **neither is in D5 today because neither was needed** |
| **G-52 applies to the door hinge side** | A device body near the hinge sweeps a smaller arc than one near the latch. **Spacing that assumes the door is stationary has ignored the swing** |

**The flexing loop is the one real addition and it is a D5 question, not a D3 one. It
adds no conductors. It adds a length allowance and a fixing, to five existing ones.**
