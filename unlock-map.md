# The unlock map. What F-106 and M-02 actually buy.

**2026-09-05. Five of eleven documents exist: D2, D4, D5, D6, D10.** This is what
the remaining six wait on, and what closing the two open gates would release.

## The short answer

| | |
|---|---|
| **F-106, the terminal survey** | **Unblocks all 125 joints in D4 and 128 cells in D5. It releases no NEW document.** It converts two documents from unusable to usable |
| **M-02, the wall** | **Releases D3 and the last of D6, and it is the only gate on either.** It is the one that turns documents into buildable pages |
| **Neither gate** | **D7 and D11 can start now. D8 can be drafted now** |
| **Nothing unlocks D9** | It is a record of a commissioning that has not happened |

**So the evening that buys the most is M-02.** F-106 makes what exists WORK; M-02
makes NEW documents POSSIBLE.

## Document by document

### D1, the build book. STARTABLE NOW, in part. Not gated on F-106 or M-02.

**Depends on** the frozen FL- and M- rows and the schedules. **What it can carry
today:** the standpipe method, D-121 and D-131, complete and figure-free; the two
tanks' overflow and air gap requirements; the pump box and display box entry faces;
the assembly order the 1st Edition set supplied. **What it cannot:** anything with a
dimension on it, which is M-02 and the float heights.

**It is the biggest single piece of unwritten work and none of it is blocked.**

### D3, the enclosure layout sheets. GATED ON M-02, AND ONLY ON M-02.

**The main panel's faces exist** - five 22 mm holes on top, cord grips on the bottom,
D-146's allocation, D-110 and F-088 on the top face. **What is missing is where
anything sits, which is geometry, which is the wall.**

**This is the document M-02 buys outright.**

### D7, the purchase package. STARTABLE NOW. Gated on neither.

**order.md holds MAIN-PANEL's list as requirements and every subsystem has returned
search terms.** What it needs is the owner's lookups, which are already how this
project works.

**And it has a live warning attached: the parallel build bought seven DIN-rail
grounding terminal blocks before the ground bar decision and is returning them.**
G-41 binds here - **a supplementing purchase list re-buys what the base already has,
and this is the document where that costs money.**

### D8, the commissioning checklist. DRAFTABLE NOW. Its first stage is gated on F-106.

**commissioning.md is its source register and holds all 24 C- rows.** The checklist
form, the stage gating and the prerequisites can be written today.

**What waits: its first stage checks landed conductors against the schedule, and
that stage cannot be run until D4 is unblocked.** So D8 can be WRITTEN now and
cannot be RUN until F-106 closes.

### D9, the baseline and maintenance record. NOT GATED - EMPTY BY DEFINITION.

**Depends on D8 entirely, and its content cannot exist before commissioning runs.**
The FORM can be written now. **Nothing unlocks its content except doing the
commissioning.**

### D11, the channel register. EXISTS AS A LIVE RECORD WITH EMPTY COLUMNS.

**Depends on C-09, which binds product and role to a channel and is first in the
commissioning order.** channel-register.md is already the file.

**Not gated on F-106 or M-02.** Gated on C-09, which is gated on the build existing.

## What closing each gate actually buys

**F-106, one evening with the parts and a pen:**

- **D4 goes from 125 blocked joints to buildable.** Every joint on every page.
- **D5's 128 empty terminal cells fill**, which is 65 percent of its remaining
  empties.
- **D8's first stage becomes runnable.**
- **It releases no new document**, and that is the honest answer: it makes two
  finished documents usable rather than producing a third.

**M-02, measuring the wall:**

- **D3 becomes writable at all.** It is gated on nothing else.
- **D6's last position and spacing cells fill**, which is the whole remaining
  balance of the cable schedule.
- **D1 can carry its dimensioned half.**
- **RUN- lengths become computable** under D-090's cut rule, wall run plus 3 ft -
  and until then no page implies a route, which is why D5 has no route column.

## The one thing neither gate touches

**D1 is the largest unwritten document and it is blocked on nothing.** It can be
started tonight, without the survey and without the wall, and it will still be
incomplete in exactly the places M-02 fills.

**If the question is what to do with an evening: the survey buys usable documents,
the wall buys new ones, and D1 needs neither and nobody has started it.**
